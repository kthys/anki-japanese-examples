
*japaneseDstField*: Field to put the Japanese part of the example sentences.

*translationDstField*: Field to put the translation of the example sentences.

*audioDstField*: Field to put the audio recording of the example sentences.

*maxSentenceOptions*: How many sentence options to show in the selection dialog when adding an example manually (1-100, default 30). Higher values fetch more pages from Tatoeba and include rarer sentences, but take longer.
